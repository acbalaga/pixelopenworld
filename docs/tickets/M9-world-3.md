### POW-M9-08 The Crown

Size: L Depends: M9-01 Status: DRAFT

Goal: tier 8 central highland. A ruined capital with no hub.
Scope: template adjusted: no hub, no shops, a handful of hermit NPCs, the hardest dungeons, the final regional boss and its non-combat resolution. Floating stone traversal. The guestbook prop is placed (content in M10).
Acceptance: template adjusted. The Crown is survivable only with tier 7+ gear or equivalent non-combat mastery (fight-sim and scripted non-combat runs).

### POW-M9-09 The Undercroft

Size: L (split per depth) Depends: M9-01, M4-08 Status: DRAFT

Goal: ten depths under the Continent, entered from every region.
Scope: connected dungeon network with its own tile set that shifts with depth. Entrances in all eight regions (earlier regions get theirs added here). 12 Waystones. Deepest bosses. Sealed rooms reserved for M10 Truth locations.
Acceptance: dungeon validator passes for the whole network. Every depth reachable. Sealed rooms are present and inert.
